Privacy Policy

How Zelanix Ag handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily submit through the contact form, including your name, work email, company, phone number, product category, project details, and related information.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential projects, communicate about services, and maintain appropriate business records.

Form Processing

The website contact form may be processed by Formspree or another form service. Information submitted through the form is therefore also subject to that provider's privacy and security practices.

Confidentiality

We treat project information with care, but a website form should not be used to transmit highly sensitive proprietary information. A formal confidentiality agreement can be completed before detailed technical information is exchanged.

Cookies and Analytics

If analytics or other tracking services are added, this policy should be updated to identify those services and any cookies they use.
