Field Research for Agricultural Innovators

Independent field research and technical support for companies moving agricultural technologies from development toward commercialization.

Built Around the Organizations We Serve

Zelanix Ag works with agricultural innovators that need reliable field evidence to make advancement, positioning, regulatory, commercialization, and market-entry decisions. Our role is tailored to the development stage, business objective, and evidence each organization needs to move its technology forward.

Biological, Biostimulant & Inoculant Developers

For companies advancing microbial products, biostimulants, biofertilizers, inoculants, and other biologically based technologies, Zelanix Ag provides field research that helps establish performance, consistency, use strategy, and commercial fit under relevant production conditions.

  • Proof of concept
  • Multi-location validation
  • Rate and timing strategy
  • Product positioning
  • Program integration
  • Commercial demonstrations
