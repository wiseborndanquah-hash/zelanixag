OUR STORY

Built to Strengthen the Transition From Discovery to Field Development

Promising agricultural innovations often begin with strong scientific potential, but moving from laboratory or greenhouse research into reliable field performance introduces a new set of challenges.

Limited internal field capacity, unsuitable trial locations, inconsistent research execution, unclear protocols, variable environments, regulatory requirements, and gaps in data interpretation can increase development costs, delay decisions, and make it difficult to determine a technology’s true potential.

Zelanix Ag was established to help bridge that gap.

Our Role

We combine scientific rigor, practical field experience, flexible research networks, and commercial perspective to help agricultural innovators design better studies, strengthen trial execution, generate reliable data, and translate research findings into confident development decisions.

Our goal is to help clients reduce development risk, avoid unnecessary delays and costs, and move promising technologies from research toward successful field application and commercialization.

MISSION

Generate Reliable Evidence and Reduce Development Risk

Our mission is to help agricultural innovators move promising technologies toward practical and commercial success through rigorous field research, reliable data, and confident scientific decisions.

VISION

A Trusted Scientific Partner for Agricultural Innovation

Our vision is to be a trusted scientific partner helping agricultural innovations prove their value in the field, advance with confidence, and deliver meaningful benefits to growers and agriculture.

OUR OPERATING MODEL

Senior Led, Flexible, and Built Around Your Research Program.

Zelanix Ag operates through a senior-led, project-based model designed to match the right expertise, research environment, and execution resources to each client program.

Rather than limiting projects to the capabilities of a single research location, we assemble the team and network needed to address the specific scientific, technical, geographic, and operational requirements of each study.

Depending on the program, this may include collaboration with:

  • Contract research organizations (CROs)
  • Universities and extension specialists
  • Growers and independent researchers
  • Regulatory specialists
  • Data analysts and statisticians
  • Technical and subject-matter consultants

Throughout the program, Zelanix Ag provides direct scientific oversight, coordinated project management, quality-focused execution, and a clear point of accountability.

Wiseborn B. Danquah, Ph.D.

Dr. Wiseborn B. Danquah is an agricultural scientist and field development leader with more than 20 years of experience spanning agronomy, crop protection, biologicals, plant pathology, nematology, regulatory research, and agricultural product development.

He has designed and led multi-location studies, developed research programs, coordinated CROs and university collaborators, trained research partners, supported regulated field studies, and translated research findings into product development, positioning, and commercialization decisions.

His hands-on approach helps ensure that each program is scientifically rigorous, operationally practical, and aligned with the client's development objectives.

  • 20+ years of agricultural research and development experience

CONSULTANT

Kayimbi M. Tubajika, Ph.D.

Regulatory Strategy & Plant Pathology

His career has included work with USDA APHIS PPQ, where he has contributed scientific expertise to pest risk assessments and regulatory analyses used to evaluate plant health risks associated with agricultural commodities and pathways of pest introduction.

At Zelanix Ag, Dr. Tubajika provides expertise in regulatory science, plant pathology, and regulatory strategy, helping clients connect rigorous plant-health science with practical product development and regulatory decisions.
