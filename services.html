Field Trial Strategy and Management

Successful field research requires more than placing trials at several locations. Every program must connect the research question, experimental design, trial environment, partner capability, operational execution, data quality, and final decision.

Zelanix Ag helps clients build and manage field programs that produce reliable and useful evidence.

Services May Include

  • Research objectives and decision criteria
  • Protocol preparation and technical review
  • Experimental design and randomization
  • Treatment, assessment, and sampling plans
  • Crop, geography, and site selection
  • CRO, university, grower, and consultant identification
  • Proposal, budget, scope, and timeline review
  • Trial material and application coordination
  • Research partner training and trial monitoring
  • Protocol compliance and data quality review
  • Statistical and agronomic interpretation
  • Final technical reporting
Client outcome: A coordinated program that reduces avoidable execution risk and generates data appropriate for the decision.

Technical Development and Product Positioning

A product may perform well in an early study but still require substantial development before it is ready for broader investment or commercialization.

We help clients understand where a product performs, how it should be used, what factors influence performance, and what additional evidence is required.

Services May Include

  • Proof of concept and product validation
  • Rate, timing, placement, and delivery optimization
  • Use pattern development
  • Crop and regional fit assessment
  • Competitive and standard program comparisons
  • Tank mix and program integration studies
  • Product performance troubleshooting
  • Biological product evaluation
  • Commercial demonstration planning
  • Grower and technical partner feedback
  • Product positioning recommendations
  • Commercial readiness and evidence gap assessment
Client outcome: Clearer understanding of product performance, target uses, risks, evidence gaps, and advancement opportunities.

Regulatory Field Study and Stewardship Support

Zelanix Ag supports the scientific execution, documentation, and stewardship of regulated field research, helping align study objectives with permit conditions, field procedures, material accountability, monitoring, and partner coordination.

Services May Include

  • Regulatory field study planning and permit support
  • Site, partner, and compliance documentation
  • Protocol review and research-partner training
  • Regulated-material stewardship and accountability
  • Confinement, monitoring, sampling, and traceability support
  • CRO, regulatory-team, and field-partner coordination
  • Corrective actions and study closeout documentation

USDA PPQ-526 Permit Regulated Field Trials

Support for site planning, trial establishment, regulated-material stewardship, documentation, monitoring, training, partner coordination, and closeout.

USDA BRS-Regulated Field Trials

BRS-aligned support for site coordination, regulated-material handling, compliance documentation, field-team training, monitoring, traceability, and operational closeout.

Scope note: Zelanix Ag strengthens the field science, stewardship, documentation, and execution of regulated programs. We do not replace legal counsel or a full-service regulatory registration consultancy.

West Africa Technical Development & Product Positioning

Zelanix Ag supports agricultural technology companies seeking to evaluate, adapt, and position products for West African production systems. We help translate technologies developed in other markets into locally relevant field research programs that generate the evidence needed for product-development, positioning, and market-entry decisions.

Services May Include

  • West Africa field research strategy and trial planning
  • Identification and coordination of suitable research sites and local partners
  • Product validation under representative crops, environments, and production systems
  • Evaluation of rates, timing, application methods, and use strategies
  • Multi-location performance and environmental adaptation studies
  • Comparative testing against locally relevant standards and practices
  • Technical interpretation and product-positioning recommendations
  • Regulatory and stewardship coordination where applicable
  • Technical support for commercialization and market-entry planning
Client outcome: Evidence not simply that a product works, but where, how, and under what conditions it can deliver meaningful value in West African production systems.

Data Interpretation and Scientific Communication

Field trials create value only when their results lead to better decisions. A statistical output alone may not establish whether a response is biologically meaningful, commercially relevant, or consistent enough to support advancement.

Services May Include

  • Data completeness and quality review
  • Statistical analysis and agronomic interpretation
  • Cross location performance evaluation
  • Variability and outlier assessment
  • Environmental response interpretation
  • Technical and executive summaries
  • Development recommendations
  • Product use and positioning guidance
  • Leadership, investor, and partner presentations
  • Technical training and communication materials
Client outcome: Clear reporting that explains what happened, why it matters, and what the organization should do next.
RESEARCH CAPABILITIES

Experience Across the Technologies Shaping Modern Agriculture

Zelanix Ag supports field research across biological, chemical, seed, plant health, agronomic, genetic, digital, and emerging agricultural technologies. Every research program is designed around the product, target pest or pathogen, production system, environment, application strategy, and development decision.

Crop Protection & Biocontrol Research +

Field evaluation of conventional, biological, and integrated pest-management technologies for efficacy, crop safety, application strategy, and product positioning.

  • Fungicides/biofungicides, and disease-management programs
  • Insecticides/bioinsecticides, and insect-management programs
  • Nematicides/bionematicides, and nematode-management programs
  • Herbicides and weed-management programs
  • Beneficial microorganisms and microbial consortia
  • Crop tolerance, phytotoxicity, residual activity, and performance
  • Product compatibility and integrated pest-management strategies
Inoculant, Biostimulant & Plant Nutrition Research +

Evaluation of technologies designed to protect seed, improve crop establishment, enhance plant health, increase nutrient-use efficiency, and strengthen crop performance.

  • Chemical and biological seed treatments
  • Microbial inoculants and symbiotic technologies
  • Biostimulants and biofertilizers
  • Fertilizers and specialty nutrition products
  • Macronutrient and micronutrient technologies
  • Nutrient-use-efficiency products
  • Plant growth regulators
  • Root-health and soil-health technologies
Variety, Hybrid, Trait & Crop Performance Research +

Field research supporting genetic advancement, agronomic characterization, regional adaptation, and commercial positioning.

  • Variety and hybrid performance trials
  • Experimental lines and germplasm evaluation
  • Trait-performance studies
  • Yield and quality evaluation
  • Agronomic adaptation
  • Environmental stability
  • Pest and disease response
  • Abiotic stress tolerance
  • Standability and maturity
  • Regional performance
  • Comparative performance and positioning
Application Technology & Agronomic Management Research +

Research designed to determine how application method, formulation, timing, placement, and production practices influence product and crop performance under real-world conditions.

  • Application timing, rate, placement, and sequencing
  • Foliar, soil, in-furrow, seed treatment, drench, broadcast, and banded applications
  • Chemigation and fertigation
  • Formulation comparisons
  • Adjuvant research
  • Spray-volume, coverage, and delivery studies
  • Application-system compatibility
  • Tank mixtures and program approaches
  • Rate-response studies
  • Yield, quality, and economic-performance assessments
Agricultural Technology Field Validation +

Independent field evaluation of new and emerging technologies designed to improve agricultural productivity, efficiency, monitoring, and decision-making.

  • Precision agriculture technologies
  • Sensors and field-monitoring systems
  • Digital agronomy platforms
  • Decision-support technologies
  • Variable-rate technologies
  • Crop-monitoring systems
  • Novel application and delivery systems
  • Proof-of-concept and prototype field validation

Across every research area, Zelanix Ag designs studies to answer the questions that drive product-development and commercialization decisions.
